A full-day hackathon dedicated to the Magento and Mage-OS ecosystem. Participants pick projects, form groups based on their skills, and work together on modules, fixes, documentation and integrations that benefit the whole community.

Aimed at anyone working with Magento or Mage-OS who wants to make a concrete contribution to the platform and the community around it. Organised by Gianluca Porta, Davide Lunardon, Alessandro Ronchi and Samuele Martini.

**When:** Friday, September 25, 2026, 10:00 - 17:00 CEST (UTC+2)

**Where:** Reverse, Viale A. Silvani 6A, 40122 Bologna, Italy
